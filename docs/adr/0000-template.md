# ADR-NNNN: <decision in a short noun phrase>

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** <agent or person>

## Context

What forced a decision. Include the constraint that makes this non-obvious —
usually the five-hour budget or a requirement id.

## Decision

What we are doing. One or two sentences, in the active voice.

## Consequences

**Good:** what this buys.

**Bad:** what it costs, and what it makes harder later.

**Reversible?** If we are wrong, how expensive is the change? Prefer reversible
decisions under time pressure.

## Requirement link

Which `REQ-NN` forced this, if any. A decision that exceeds the complexity budget
must name the requirement that forced it.
